How to run it

The week

Days 1 and 2 are the main-lift days: the two competition lifts, warm-up, and nothing else. They should fit in about an hour. Days 3 and 4 are the accessory days, done back to back. Saturday leads with a squat variation and a deadlift variation; Sunday with a bench variation (pin OHP in Strength II). Each phase has its own variations, loaded by RPE, then a pull, a shoulder exercise and a few extras. Accessory days don’t count toward the tonnage progression. The accessory list stays the same all cycle; deload weeks use half sets, and the final deload has none.

  • Keep at least 48 hours between Day 1 and Day 2.
  • Do the lower accessory day first. It sits 2 days after deadlift and 3 days before squat, which keeps the squat and deadlift variations away from both heavy days.
  • The upper day sits two days before Day 1, plenty for overhead press to recover.
  • Zone 2 after either accessory day is fine if you want the extra ride.
  • If a week gets squeezed, drop the upper accessory day first, then the accessory list on the lower day. Keep the squat variation if you can.
Warm-up

Main-lift days (about 12 minutes): five minutes easy on the bike, then the McGill big three — curl-up, side plank, bird dog — in short holds of about 10 seconds, descending reps (for example 5-3-1 per side). Add hip airplanes or 90/90 switches, 5 per side.

Ramp the first main lift: bar ×10, 40% ×5, 60% ×3, 75% ×2, 85% ×1 before anything heavier. The second main lift needs only two or three ramp sets.

Accessory days: bike and the McGill big three, then two or three ramp sets for each variation.

Protecting your back
  • Squat and deadlift are never heavy on the same day. All loaded hinging happens on Day 2, so your erectors get 4–5 days between hard exposures.
  • Saturday’s deadlift variation (RDL, pause deadlift, rack pull from just below the knee) is 2–3 sets, capped at RPE 7, done after the squat variation.
  • Belt on every set at or above 80%.
  • Deadlift is capped at RPE 8.5 until test day. Stop any set when the brace leaks; that rep doesn’t count anyway.
  • No heavy hinging in the first hour after waking.
  • If your back feels off during warm-up, swap that day’s deadlift for block pulls just below the knee or trap bar at the same RPE, and drop Saturday’s deadlift variation that week. If it spasms, run that week as a deload rather than training through it.
  • The McGill work stays in the warm-up even when you’re short on time. It’s the cheapest insurance in the program.
Running a session
  1. Walk in with the e1RM you believe today (the numbers at the top).
  2. The top set’s load is planned from it for the target reps and RPE, shown in grey in the box.
  3. Do the top set and enter the weight, reps and the RPE it actually took.
  4. The page calculates the day’s e1RM from that set.
  5. Back-offs are percentages of the day’s e1RM, shown as the two loadable weights either side when it doesn’t land on 2.5 kg. Pick the lower or higher by feel.

Example: last bench was 115 × 5 @ 7.5, e1RM 143.8. Next goal is 5 @ 8, which plans to 116.6, so load 117.5. It takes RPE 8.5: 117.5 × 5 @ 8.5 is a day e1RM of 143.0, and 75% is 107.2, so 105 or 107.5.

Tap “Use as e1RM” to carry a day’s number into the next session. Leave it alone if the day was off for reasons that won’t repeat. Back-offs on deload weeks use the e1RM at the top of the page. Accessory-day variations are prescribed by RPE only; choose the weight on the day.

Cycling alongside

Cardio takes Monday, Wednesday and Friday: Zone 2 on Monday and Wednesday, intervals on Friday. Monday and Wednesday each fall the day before a main-lift day, so they stay easy; Friday is the only cardio day not within 24 hours of Day 1 or Day 2. Optional Zone 2 after the Saturday and Sunday accessory sessions.

In week 26, shorten the interval session. From April 19: Zone 2 only, 45 minutes or less, and nothing on April 26.

Test day

Order: squat, bench, overhead press, deadlift. Three attempts each, 5–8 minutes between attempts, and about 15 minutes between lifts. Use the same warm-up and ramp as the openers in week 27.

Run it like a meet: squat to honest depth, pause bench on a spotter’s “press” command, record every attempt. Take the second attempt off how the opener moved: fast opener, go to the planned second or a bit above; grindy opener, repeat-plus-5.
